Standard Operating Procedure

Core Ingredients (Final Volume: 100ml)

  • Ethanol (95%) 83.3 ml
  • Hydrogen Peroxide (3%) 4.17 ml
  • Glycerol (98%) 1.45 ml
  • Distilled Water Up to 100 ml

Process Flow

  1. Clean and disinfect the working area.
  2. Accurately measure the required quantities of each ingredient.
  3. Carefully mix the formulation in a laboratory beaker.
  4. Infuse with fresh Neem leaves.
  5. Store safely for final settling.

Transparency & Safety: What's Inside?

We believe you have the right to know exactly what you are putting on your skin. Here is why we chose each ingredient.

Ethanol (95%) Active Defense

Why we use it: This is the core antimicrobial agent. At this exact concentration, it effectively destroys the cell walls of 99.9% of harmful bacteria and viruses.

Safety: Highly safe for external skin application, though it evaporates quickly.

Hydrogen Peroxide (3%) Purifier

Why we use it: It is not actually for your hands! It is used to inactivate contaminating bacterial spores that might be present in the solution or the beaker during the lab process.

Safety: Ensures the sanitizer you use is sterile and completely safe.

Glycerol (98%) Skin Protector

Why we use it: Pure alcohol can severely dry out human skin. Glycerol acts as a humectant—meaning it draws moisture into your skin, counteracting the drying effect of the ethanol.

Safety: Extremely safe, deeply hydrating, and gentle on sensitive skin.

Neem Leaves (Azadirachta indica) Natural Booster

Why we use it: Our unique herbal innovation. Neem adds natural anti-inflammatory and skin-soothing properties, reducing harshness while providing a secondary layer of botanical antimicrobial defense.

Safety: 100% natural, non-toxic, and traditionally used for skin health.
